# Literature Review

## Overview

The literature review focuses on the use of Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Blockchain and digital technologies in public governance and administrative decision-making.

---

## 1. Zuiderwijk, Chen & Salem (2021)

**Focus:** Artificial Intelligence in public governance

**Key Finding:**  
AI has significant potential to improve public governance, but further empirical, explanatory and multidisciplinary research is required.

**Relevance to Project:**  
Supports the use of AI/ML for governance-related decision support.

---

## 2. Madan & Ashok (2023)

**Focus:** AI adoption in public administration

**Key Finding:**  
AI adoption is influenced by technological, organizational and environmental factors.

**Relevance to Project:**  
Highlights the importance of considering organizational and implementation challenges.

---

## 3. Agbabiaka, Ojo & Connolly (2025)

**Focus:** Trustworthy AI-enabled public decision-making

**Key Finding:**  
AI-based public decision-making requires accountability, transparency, context sensitivity and appropriate safeguards.

**Relevance to Project:**  
Supports the human-in-the-loop approach of our system.

---

## 4. Almi’ani et al. (2026)

**Focus:** Blockchain in government

**Key Finding:**  
Blockchain can improve transparency, traceability and trust, while interoperability, regulation and organizational readiness remain challenges.

**Relevance to Project:**  
Supports the proposed audit/blockchain layer for selected administrative records.

---

## 5. AI in Public Sector Review (2025)

**Focus:** Benefits and challenges of AI in the public sector

**Key Finding:**  
AI can support automation, prediction and resource allocation, but ethical, governance and skill-related challenges must be addressed.

**Relevance to Project:**  
Supports responsible use of AI for administrative analytics.

---

## 6. Blockchain and E-Government Research

**Focus:** Blockchain adoption in e-government

**Key Finding:**  
Blockchain adoption in government can provide benefits such as transparency and traceability, but challenges include scalability, governance and interoperability.

**Relevance to Project:**  
Provides the basis for considering blockchain as an audit mechanism.

---

## 7. Blockchain for Public Services

**Focus:** Blockchain applications in public services

**Key Finding:**  
Blockchain can support transparency, trust and secure handling of selected public-service transactions.

**Relevance to Project:**  
Motivates the use of a blockchain-backed audit layer.

---

## 8. AI-Based Decision Support Research

**Focus:** AI-assisted administrative decision-making

**Key Finding:**  
AI can help identify patterns, prioritize cases and provide analytical recommendations.

**Relevance to Project:**  
Supports the proposed administrative dashboard and decision-support workflow.

---

## 9. Digital Transformation in Public Administration

**Focus:** Digital technologies in government

**Key Finding:**  
Digital transformation can improve administrative efficiency, but organizational readiness and implementation challenges remain important.

**Relevance to Project:**  
Supports an integrated digital platform for administrative analytics.

---

## 10. Trust and Accountability in AI Governance

**Focus:** Responsible AI in public-sector applications

**Key Finding:**  
Transparency, accountability and human oversight are important when AI is used to support public-sector decisions.

**Relevance to Project:**  
Supports the project's human-in-the-loop decision-making model.

---

# Research Gap

Existing research frequently studies AI, IoT and blockchain as separate technologies.

There is an opportunity to demonstrate a student-scale prototype that integrates:

**Data Collection → Data Processing → AI/ML Analytics → Auditability → Administrative Dashboard**

The proposed project addresses this integration gap through a unified governance decision-support workflow.

---

# Literature Review Summary

| Technology | Identified Contribution |
|---|---|
| AI / ML | Prediction, classification and anomaly detection |
| IoT / APIs | Timely data collection |
| Blockchain | Traceability and auditability |
| Dashboards | Visualization and decision support |
| Human Oversight | Accountability in AI-assisted decisions |

## Key Insight

**AI provides intelligence, while the audit layer provides traceability and the dashboard converts analytical results into actionable information.**
